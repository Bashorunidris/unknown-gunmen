HOW TO PLAY

Phone: left side = joystick (push fully forward to sprint) · right side = swipe to aim (or drag while holding FIRE) · AIM zooms the optic · SLIDE while sprinting, then JUMP and SLIDE again to chain slide-hops.

Battle Royale: you start with a pistol. Walk over ammo, healing and armor to take it; use PICK UP for weapons. BAND / MED / PLATE use your supplies. If you are downed, your squad can revive you (up to 4 times: 4s, 7s, 20s, then 60s). Stand next to a downed teammate and hold USE to revive them.

Keyboard: WASD · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch · Ctrl/X slide · R reload · Q swap · Right-click aim · E use · 1/2/3 heal · Esc settings.
